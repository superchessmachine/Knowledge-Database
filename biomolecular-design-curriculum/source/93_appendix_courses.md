## Appendix B — The Enumerated Courses

The second edition named ten courses as important and left every one of them as a
bare playlist link — the exact failure it criticized elsewhere. This appendix is
the repair. Every lecture, with its real title, runtime and link.

**Three things worth knowing before the tables.**

**First, a discovery that resolves a long-standing confusion.** *Jeffrey Gray's
JHU course and the "IPD lecture series" enumerated in Appendix A.7 are the same
twelve recordings.* Same video IDs, same guest lecturers, same order. They are
cross-posted, and anyone who has seen both lists has been looking at one course
twice. **Those twelve lectures are the whole published set** — graylab.jhu.edu's
own video page confirms the rest of the fourteen-week semester was never
recorded.

**Second, several of these courses are incomplete and the gaps are listed.**
Lindahl has re-recorded his course five times and only the 2016 edition is
complete; the missing lectures in 2017, 2019 and 2020 are named below rather
than papered over.

**Third, two counts that look wrong are correct.** The Chodera workshop really
has exactly 43 videos — and its real title is *Workshop on Free Energy, Kinetics,
and MSM in Drug Design 2018*, not anything to do with Alchemistry. The PLUMED
Masterclass stops at 2022; there is no 23.x series.
